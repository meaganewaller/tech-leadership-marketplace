# History Report Template

```markdown
## Communication history: [Name]
**Last contact:** [date] ([n] weeks ago) · **Cadence in profile:** [cadence]

### Conflicts with the current draft
- **[What the draft says]** contradicts **[what was said]**, told [date].
  [How to frame the revision.]

[Omit this section when there is no draft to check. Never omit it when
there is a conflict -- it goes first, always.]

### Open commitments
| Commitment | Promised | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [what] | [date] | [date] | [open / overdue by n days] |

### What they've been told
| Date | Topic | In short |
|---|---|---|
| [date] | [topic] | [one line] |

### Claims still standing
- [claim, with its hedge intact] — said [date]
```

## Worked example

```markdown
## Communication history: Priya Nadar
**Last contact:** 2026-08-12 (5 weeks ago) · **Cadence in profile:** monthly

### Conflicts with the current draft
- **Draft says "on track for early December."** On 2026-08-12 you told her
  "should land in October, assuming the vendor's sandbox is ready in
  September." The date moved and she hasn't been told.
  Lead with the revision: name October, say it's now early December, and
  say the vendor sandbox arrived four weeks late.

### Open commitments
| Commitment | Promised | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-customer cost breakdown once the pricing model is built | 2026-08-12 | 2026-09-01 | overdue by 19 days |

### What they've been told
| Date | Topic | In short |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-12 | payments migration | Migration plan, October target, ~$200k/year estimated saving |
| 2026-06-30 | Q3 planning | Payments migration prioritized over the reporting rebuild |

### Claims still standing
- "Estimated $200k/year saved on instance costs, at current traffic" — said 2026-08-12
```

What the example is doing, and why:

- **The conflict goes first and is stated flatly.** Not "you may want to
  mention," not buried under the commitments table. The whole skill earns
  its place on this one section.
- **The previous claim is quoted with its hedge** -- "assuming the vendor's
  sandbox is ready in September." That hedge is the honest framing of the
  revision, and it's available only because the log kept it.
- **The overdue commitment surfaces even though the draft is about the
  timeline.** It's unrelated, it's three weeks late, and it will land badly
  if she has to ask.
- **The standing $200k claim is listed but not flagged.** It hasn't been
  contradicted. Listing it is how the next update avoids restating it as
  though it were new, and how a revision to *it* gets caught later.
